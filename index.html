Topic 2.1: Envelope Generators — How a Note Changes Over Time

An envelope gives a synth note the shape of a real instrument. Select an instrument to inspect its envelope curve (Figures 3–10) and audition how its loudness evolves across Attack, Decay, Sustain, and Release.

▲ Loudness (up the side) Time (across the bottom) ►
Attack Fast (full level)
Decay Falls to 60%
Sustain Holds level (60%)
Release Falls to silence
Simulates pressing key, holding, and letting go.

Figure 3: Generic ADSR envelope

Loudness rises to full at Attack, falls to about 60 percent at Decay, holds during Sustain while the key is down, then falls to silence during Release.

Analogy: a hand clap. A clap is sharp at the start, drops quickly, and fades away. An envelope is the shape of that clap, applied to each note.
Key question to ask: Does the sound keep going while you hold the key? If yes, set Sustain high. If the sound fades by itself, set Sustain low and use Decay to control the fade.
